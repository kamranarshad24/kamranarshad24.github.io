Marketing for plumbers: more of the calls that turn into paid jobs

Kamran ArshadUpdated 30 Oct 2026
United KingdomUnited StatesCanadaAustralia

Plumbing marketing comes down to being visible at the moment something leaks, and answering fast. Someone with a leak is unlikely to spend long comparing options.

I run Google Ads, Local Services Ads, the Google Maps profile and service pages for a UK carpet cleaning company, with calls and forms tracked. That is the system I would bring to a plumbing company.

Where I'd start for a plumbing company

01

Google Ads for urgent and high-value work

Emergency call-outs, boiler or water heater work and bathroom installs behave very differently. Each gets its own ad groups, so you can bid hard on the jobs you want and leave out the ones you don't.

02

Local Services Ads where they're available

These charge per lead instead of per click and can appear prominently in Search. I have hands-on experience here: I set up the account and run Local Services Ads for a UK carpet cleaning company.

03

Google Maps and your Business Profile

Categories, services, service areas, photos and a steady flow of reviews decide how often you appear when someone nearby searches for a plumber.

04

A page for each service and area

One homepage can't rank for drain unblocking, leak repair and five nearby towns. Separate pages give Google and your customer an exact match.

05

Tracking down to the booked job

A tap on your phone number isn't a customer. Connected calls and forms are tracked, and booked jobs can be sent back to Google Ads so it learns which searches bring real work.

Questions worth asking any plumbing marketing agency

  1. Who owns the ad account?It should be you, so your history stays with you if you ever leave.
  2. Are leads shared with other plumbers?Some lead services sell one enquiry to several companies. Your own ads and pages only bring leads to you.
  3. What counts as a conversion?Ask whether it means a connected call, or just a tap on a phone number.
  4. Can I see the search terms?You should be able to see exactly what people searched before they called.
