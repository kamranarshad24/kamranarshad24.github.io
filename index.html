I've rebuilt a mismanaged Google Ads account for a dental clinic, run Meta and Snapchat for fashion e-commerce, and redesigned Shopify stores for conversion.

The thread running through all of it is measurement. I'm the person who finds the broken pixel, the duplicate conversion, the budget quietly leaking into the Display Network.
