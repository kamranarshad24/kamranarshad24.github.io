Google Ads management where every lead is tracked back to its keyword

Kamran ArshadUpdated 10 Oct 2026
United KingdomUnited StatesCanadaAustralia

I rebuild and run Google Ads accounts for small businesses, with form submissions and phone calls tracked back to the keyword that produced them.

You get a short plain-language update at the end of every week, and a free 15-minute audit before you commit to anything.

What you get

01

A clean account structure

Search and Display kept apart, location targeting set to the areas you actually serve, and a negative keyword list that filters out searches with no buying intent.

02

Calls and forms counted as conversions

Google Tag Manager triggers for form submissions and click-to-call, imported into Google Ads so bidding can optimise toward enquiries.

03

Booked jobs fed back as revenue

For a UK carpet cleaning business I built a plugin that captures the Google click ID at form submission and sends the real job value back to Google Ads as an offline conversion.

Proof from real accounts

Case study

Smile Line Dental, Islamabad

Before: worldwide targeting, Search and Display mixed in the same campaigns, no negative keywords, and neither form submissions nor calls tracked.

After: a Search-only account targeted to Islamabad, with a negative keyword list and Google Tag Manager tracking. For the first time the clinic could see which ads and keywords produced patient enquiries.
