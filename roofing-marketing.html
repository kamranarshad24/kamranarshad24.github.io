Roofing marketing: fewer shared leads, more signed jobs from your own channels

Kamran ArshadUpdated 30 Oct 2026
United KingdomUnited StatesCanadaAustralia

A roof is a big decision. Homeowners often compare several quotes and take time to choose, so a low cost per lead means little if those leads never sign.

I run Google Ads, Local Services Ads, the Google Maps profile and service pages for a UK carpet cleaning company, with booked jobs sent back to Google Ads. A roofing account would use the same system.

What I'd change first for a roofing company

01

Repairs and replacements kept apart

A leak needs someone quickly, while a full replacement is a high-value job with a longer decision. Each gets its own campaign, landing page and budget so one can't quietly eat the other's money.

02

Your own enquiries, not shared lists

Bought roofing leads can be sold to several companies at once, which turns the job into a race on price. Your own ads and pages bring the homeowner to you first.

03

Trust on the page where they decide

Real photos of your jobs, reviews, accreditations and clear service areas matter for a purchase this size. A generic template page wastes the click you paid for.

04

Google Maps and Local Services Ads

A strong Business Profile and pay-per-lead ads put you in front of people ready to call. I have hands-on experience with both, including setting up and running Local Services Ads for a UK carpet cleaning company.

05

Tracking past the first call

Long sales cycles need more than a lead count. Signed jobs and their value can be sent back to Google Ads, so it learns which searches lead to real roofs and not just quote requests.

What to ask a roofing marketing agency

  1. Are these leads exclusive?Ask directly whether any other roofer receives the same enquiry.
  2. Do you report on signed jobs?A report that stops at leads can't tell you whether the ads are profitable.
  3. Who owns the ad account and website?If you leave, your history and pages should stay with you.
  4. Will the page show my real work?Stock photos and shared templates don't build trust for a job this size.

Should roofers buy leads?

Bought leads can fill gaps quickly, but they may be shared and they stop when you stop paying. Your own ads and pages take longer to build, but the enquiries come to you alone. Many roofers use both at first.
