Meta Ads management that checks Meta's numbers against your real orders

I run Facebook and Instagram campaigns for online stores, and I reconcile what Meta reports against what actually arrived in your store.

What you get

01

A pixel and Conversions API audit before any scaling

Campaign build and management

Audience research, campaign structure, creative briefs and ongoing optimisation across Facebook and Instagram placements.

03

Results you can check

Each campaign is judged on orders you can find in your own store as well as on the ROAS Meta shows.

Proof from real accounts

Case study

The Body Essential, fashion e-commerce

Meta showed around 100 orders while Shopify showed 55 to 70. A second pixel, installed by a third-party app, was reporting purchases that never happened.

The campaigns attached to it showed the strongest ROAS in the account, so they kept receiving more budget. When I disabled that pixel, every campaign optimising toward it stopped delivering, which confirmed that roughly 30 to 40 percent of daily budget had been chasing conversions that did not exist.

AlphaMart, my own store

Over eight months I ran my own store on Snapchat, Meta and TikTok. Meta spent roughly PKR 15,000 on campaigns that never produced a sale, and they stayed on because nobody was checking below the account-level number. Common questions

Do you manage Instagram ads as well as Facebook?

Yes. I run Meta campaigns across Facebook and Instagram placements.

Why do Meta and Shopify show different order counts?

Some difference is normal because the two platforms count on different rules. A large gap usually points to a tracking fault such as a duplicate pixel. On one account Meta reported around 100 orders against 55 to 70 in Shopify.

What do you need from me to get started?

Access to your Meta ad account, pixel and store as an admin or analyst, which I will guide you through, plus a short brief on your goals. That is usually enough to start within 24 to 48 hours.

Can you work with brands in the UK, US, Canada and Australia?

Yes. I work remotely from Islamabad, Pakistan, and currently have an ongoing UK client. I communicate over WhatsApp and email and reply within a few hours during my working hours.

Is this a one-off audit or ongoing management?

Either. I do one-off audits and tracking fixes, and I also manage campaigns ongoing.
