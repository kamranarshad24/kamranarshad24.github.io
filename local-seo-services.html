What you get

Proof from a UK service business

Case study

The business started with no website, no tracking and no ads. I built the site on WordPress, set up and optimised the Google Business Profile, verified Search Console and worked through its indexing issues.

Why a page per service

Searchers look for one service in one place. A dedicated page lets Google match that search to a page about exactly that service and area.

How it works

  1. Audit firstI review the Google Business Profile, the site, Search Console and the pages you already have.
  2. Build and fixI finish the profile, build the service and area pages, and clear indexing and on-page issues.
  3. Run and optimiseI track what Google is showing and adjust pages ongoing, with a short update every week.
