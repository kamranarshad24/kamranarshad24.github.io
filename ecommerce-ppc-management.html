A note on context

Context

AlphaMart sold cash-on-delivery in Pakistan. A card-paid store in the UK, US, Canada or Australia has a different funnel, so read these numbers as an example of the method and not as a benchmark for your store.

How it works

  1. Audit firstI review ad accounts, tracking and order data side by side and find where the numbers disagree.
  2. Build and fixI correct tracking and structure, then rebuild campaigns around orders you can verify.
  3. Run and optimiseI manage the channels ongoing, move budget toward what produces real orders, and send a short update every week.
