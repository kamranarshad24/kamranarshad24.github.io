Med spa marketing: more booked consultations, inside Google's and Meta's rules

Kamran ArshadUpdated 30 Oct 2026
United KingdomUnited StatesCanadaAustralia

Med spa advertising is different because the platforms restrict what you can say. A campaign written like any other can be disapproved, and repeated problems can put the whole account at risk.

I build Google and Meta campaigns with tracking from enquiry onwards. For a clinic, I would plan them around those rules from the start.

The rules that shape a med spa campaign

Checked on Google's and Meta's policy pages, October 2026

These rules change and depend on the country, so check them again before any campaign. This is practical guidance and not legal advice. Medical advertising also has its own local regulations.

01

Prescription drug terms on Google

Google restricts prescription drug terms in ads, landing pages and keywords. Campaigns targeting the US, Canada or New Zealand may use them promotionally under local law, but keyword-targeting them needs certification. Elsewhere, including the UK and Australia, promotional use isn't allowed. If a treatment uses a prescription drug, check its name against Google's list before using it in ads or on the landing page.

02

Age targeting and wording on Meta

Meta requires ads for cosmetic procedures to target people aged 18 or over. It also bars statements of inferiority about someone's appearance, so copy has to sell the outcome without attacking how the reader looks.

03

Before-and-after images

Meta's policy allows before-and-after images for general cosmetic procedures when the ad targets adults and avoids negative messaging. Check the current wording and your local medical advertising rules before using them.

What I would set up for a clinic

  1. Google Ads for treatment searchesPeople searching for a treatment near them are the closest to booking, so these campaigns are split by treatment and location.
  2. Meta Ads for local awarenessFacebook and Instagram are visual channels that suit consultations and seasonal offers, with creative written to meet the policy.
  3. A local presence on MapsA complete Business Profile, reviews and a page for each treatment help you get found by people nearby.
  4. Tracking to the consultationCalls, forms and booking requests are tracked by source, so you can see what each channel really brings.

Common questions

Can a med spa advertise on Google Ads?

Yes, but with limits. Prescription drug terms are restricted, and the rules depend on the countries you target. Campaigns need to be planned around Google's healthcare policy for your location.

Can med spas use before-and-after photos in Facebook ads?

Meta's policy allows them for general cosmetic procedures when the ad targets people aged 18 or over and avoids negative messaging about appearance. Local medical advertising rules may be stricter, so check those too.
