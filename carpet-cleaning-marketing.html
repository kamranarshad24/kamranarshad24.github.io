What usually holds carpet cleaners back

  1. Too few recent reviewsIn the towns I checked, the businesses at the top of Maps often have well over 100 reviews and keep adding more.
  2. A thin Google profileMissing categories, unlisted services or an empty service area all reduce how often Google shows you.
  3. One page for everythingA single homepage can't rank for sofa cleaning, rug cleaning and five nearby towns at the same time.
  4. No trackingWithout call and form tracking you can't tell whether Google, Ads or word of mouth is bringing in the jobs.
