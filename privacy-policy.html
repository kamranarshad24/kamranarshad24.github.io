01 Overview

This Privacy Policy explains how Kamran Arshad ("I", "me", "my") handles information collected through this website (kamranarshad24.github.io). I'm a paid ads and conversion tracking specialist based in Islamabad, Pakistan, working with clients globally.

By using this site or contacting me through it, you agree to the practices described below. If you don't agree, please don't use the site or submit your details.

02 Information I Collect

Information you give me

When you fill in the contact or "free audit" form, I collect the details you choose to share:

  • Name — so I know who I'm speaking with
  • Email address — to reply to your enquiry
  • Phone / WhatsApp number — if you provide one for a quicker response
  • Message details — anything you tell me about your business, ad accounts, or what you need help with

Information collected automatically

Like most websites, this one automatically records some technical data when you visit — such as your approximate location, browser and device type, pages viewed, and how you arrived here. This is gathered through cookies and analytics tools (see sections below).

03 How I Use Your Information

I only use your information for clear, limited purposes:

  • To respond to your enquiry and provide the free audit or service you requested
  • To communicate with you about a project we're working on together
  • To understand how visitors use the site so I can improve it
  • To measure which marketing efforts actually bring people here
I don't sell your data. Your information is never sold, rented, or traded. I don't add you to mailing lists or send marketing emails unless you've specifically asked me to.

04 Cookies & Tracking

This site uses cookies and similar technologies through Google Tag Manager, which can load tools such as Google Analytics (GA4) and advertising pixels. These help me understand traffic and measure performance.

You can control or block cookies through your browser settings at any time. Blocking them won't break the site — though some anonymous analytics may no longer be recorded. Most browsers also let you browse in a private/incognito mode that limits tracking.

05 Third-Party Services

A few trusted services help run this site. Each has its own privacy policy governing how it handles data:

  • Google (Tag Manager & Analytics) — site analytics and tag management. See Google's Privacy Policy.
  • FormSubmit — delivers contact-form submissions to my email. See FormSubmit.
  • GitHub Pages — hosts this website. See GitHub's Privacy Statement.
  • Google Fonts — loads the typefaces used on this site.

I don't control how these third parties process data and am not responsible for their practices, but I only work with reputable providers.

06 Data Retention

I keep enquiry details only as long as needed to respond to you and, where relevant, for the duration of our working relationship plus a reasonable period afterwards for records. If you'd like your details removed sooner, just ask (see Contact) and I'll delete them.
