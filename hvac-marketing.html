HVAC marketing: your own leads, through the busy season and the quiet one

Kamran ArshadUpdated 30 Oct 2026
United KingdomUnited StatesCanadaAustralia

HVAC demand swings with the weather. Marketing that runs the same all year overspends when you're fully booked and goes quiet when you most need the work.

I run Google Ads, Local Services Ads, the Google Maps profile and service pages for a UK carpet cleaning company, with calls and forms tracked. An HVAC account would use the same system, planned around your seasons.

How I'd plan the account around the seasons

01

Budgets that follow the season

Repair demand tends to rise in very hot or very cold weather. Budgets and ad schedules can be adjusted around your capacity, so you aren't paying for calls your team can't take.

02

Repairs and replacements kept apart

A broken system needs someone quickly, while a replacement buyer may compare quotes for longer and the job is usually worth more. Separate campaigns let each get the right ad, page and bid.

03

Your own leads instead of shared ones

Bought HVAC leads can be useful for filling gaps, but they may also go to your competitors. Your own ads, Maps listing and pages bring enquiries to you alone.

04

Local Services Ads and Google Maps

Pay-per-lead ads and a strong Business Profile put you in front of people ready to call. I have hands-on experience with both, including setting up and running Local Services Ads for a UK carpet cleaning company.

05

Tracking down to the booked job

Connected calls and forms are tracked by source. Booked jobs and their value can go back to Google Ads, so it learns the difference between a quick repair and a full installation.

What to check before hiring an HVAC marketing agency

  1. Do you own the accounts?Your ad account, website and call data should stay yours.
  2. Is the contract tied to results you can see?Long contracts are hard to judge without tracking you can check yourself.
  3. Are repairs and installs reported separately?A blended cost per lead hides which work actually pays.
  4. What happens in the quiet season?Ask for a plan for slow months, not just the peak.

Are HVAC leads better bought or generated?

Bought leads are quick but may be shared with competitors, and you stop getting them when you stop paying. Your own ads and pages take longer to set up, but the leads are yours. Many companies use both while their own channels build up.
