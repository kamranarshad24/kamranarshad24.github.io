Conversion tracking setup you can check against real leads and orders

Kamran ArshadUpdated 10 Oct 2026
United KingdomUnited StatesCanadaAustralia

I set up Google Tag Manager, GA4, the Meta Conversions API and server-side tracking, then compare what each platform reports with the leads and orders you actually received.

The first step is a free 15-minute audit of what is firing today.

What you get

01

Tag Manager and GA4 for forms and calls

Triggers that fire a conversion on every enquiry form submission and every tap on a click-to-call number, linked to Google Ads so bidding can learn from them.

02

Pixels and the Conversions API without duplicates

One pixel per platform, the Conversions API alongside it, and a check that the two are deduplicated, so purchase counts agree with your store.

03

Server-side tracking and offline conversions

A server container that routes conversion data independently of browser limits and ad blockers, and a way to send booked-job value back to Google Ads.

Proof from real accounts

Case study

Smile Line Dental, Islamabad

The clinic ran Google Ads with neither form submissions nor phone calls tracked. I installed Tag Manager, set up form and click-to-call triggers, and linked the events to Google Ads. For the first time the clinic could see which ads and keywords produced patient enquiries.
